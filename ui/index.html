Uploading your own questions? See the file format

Choose a UTF-8 plain text .txt file, up to 5 MB. Put one question per line, with fields separated by |.

Question|Option 1|Option 2|Option 3|Option 4|Correct option number
Which planet is closest to the Sun?|Venus|Mercury|Mars|Earth|2

Include at least two non-empty options. The last field is the correct option number, starting at 1. Do not include a header row or use | inside a question or option. Blank lines are ignored. Quizzes use up to 10 questions from your file.

      How quizroom works
